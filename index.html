¡Hola! I'm Jorge, a Film, Television and Media Studies graduate building a career across film, TV and emerging formats, with hands-on experience in Spain and the United States.

On the production side, I produced The Bear Beneath, an environmental documentary selected for the Santa Barbara International Film Festival and recognized by the Directors Guild of America. I also founded UC3M’s first student-run radio station, growing it from the ground up into a team of 100+ students. Alongside this, I represented 25,000+ students as Head of UC3M’s student government and served on the university’s governing board.

Most recently, I worked at the Consulate General of Spain in New York, where I built its digital communications strategy and developed audiovisual and digital content for audiences in the US and Spain.

I'm interested in understanding audiences, spotting where culture and technology are moving, and turning those insights into content and ideas that can travel across markets.
