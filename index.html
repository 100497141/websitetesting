About

A little more about me.

I’m a Film, Television and Media Studies graduate building a career across film, television and digital media.

My story started in Madrid, took me to Rochester, New York as an exchange student, then to Santa Barbara, and eventually back to New York professionally.

I like developing ideas, bringing people together and turning creative concepts into real projects. I’m especially interested in the point where entertainment, technology and audiences meet.

For my final degree project, I explored vertical fiction and branded storytelling as a way of understanding how new formats can create opportunities for creators, audiences and brands.
